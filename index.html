💡 Quick Start Guide — How to use this checklist ▼ Show
1
Create checklist for new policyClick "+ New Checklist" and fill in the insured's information — name, bind date, carrier, broker, and VIA Executive.
2
Select required documents (Phase 1)Check "Needed" next to each document required for this binding. As you collect them, check "Completed" — the date logs automatically.
3
Work through each phaseClick any step to mark it complete. Steps are color-coded — green means done, red means overdue. Phases 1–4 are due day of binding; Phase 5 within 7 days; Phase 6 within 30 days.
4
Print or save the PDF summaryOnce all steps are done, click "Print / Save PDF" to generate a one-page summary to attach to AMS360 or email to your supervisor.
5
Mark the file closedWhen everything is complete, click "Mark File Closed" to archive the policy and remove it from your active list.
